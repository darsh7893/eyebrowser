# Privacy Policy — eyeBROWSer

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

eyeBROWSer is a web browser for Android TV / Fire TV. This policy explains what the app does — and doesn't — do with your data.

## Data collection

**eyeBROWSer collects no personal data.** It has:

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or crash reporting
- No advertising or trackers
- No servers — the app talks to nobody except the websites you choose to visit

## Phone keyboard feature

The optional "Phone keyboard" feature runs a small web server **on the TV itself** (your local Wi-Fi network only). When you scan the QR code, your phone opens a page served directly by the TV and sends keystrokes back to it over your home network.

- Keystrokes travel only between your phone and your TV on your local network.
- Nothing is sent to the internet, stored, or logged by the app.
- The server only runs while the app is open and stops when the app closes.

## Network permissions

The app requests internet access solely to load web pages you navigate to, and Wi-Fi/network-state access solely to display the phone keyboard's local address. These are not used for any data collection.

## Your browsing

Websites you visit may set cookies or collect data according to their own policies — that is between you and those sites, exactly as with any browser.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same location.
